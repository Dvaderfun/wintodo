# ADR-022 — Launch Wintodo publicly at no charge

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-20
- **Supersedes:** the private tester group proposed for the first Store submission. [ADR-019](ADR-019-store-first-source-available-release.md) remains the distribution and licensing policy.
- **Owner acknowledgement:** the owner selected Free for launch and a direct production release without a tester group.

## Decision

Set Wintodo 1.0 to a zero retail price and make it discoverable in the public Microsoft Store. Retain manual publication after certification. There is no launch cohort, in-app purchase or paid tier in this version. A future freemium offering is a separate product decision.

## Consequences

The initial submission does not need a tester group. Changing the draft audience does not submit the app or publish it. Without a private baseline, installation from the public Store cannot prove signed customer behavior before publication. Record those tests when a signed build is available; the audience decision does not waive the artwork, licence, provenance or package checks.
