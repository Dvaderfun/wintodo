# ADR-023 — Reserve the official Wintodo brand and commercial distribution

- **Status:** accepted, 2026-11-20.
- **Owner acknowledgement:** the owner confirmed personal ownership, named
  Univera LLC as publisher, reserved the project name and monetization, and
  authorized uploading the existing backgrounds and removing disputed images.
- **Supersedes:** the pending first-party licence choices and, for the Store
  draft only, the prior evidence prerequisite in [ADR-019](ADR-019-store-first-source-available-release.md) §4.

The [source licence](../../LICENSE.md) allows free personal and internal builds
and free, distinctly branded community redistribution. The individual rights
holder controls official branding and commercial permissions. Univera LLC is
the authorized publisher. This separates source permissions from Store
customer terms and grants no rights to third-party components or imagery.

Retain the eight existing list backgrounds in the private submission draft at
the owner's direction. Their origin and redistribution rights remain unknown.
This decision does not establish ownership or justify an affirmative
representation about third-party rights. For a complaint, contact
support@univera.app, identify and remove or replace the disputed image, rebuild
both architectures and update the Store submission. Public source release and
certification/publication are separate from staging the draft.
