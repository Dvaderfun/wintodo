# Wintodo source licence

The [Wintodo Source-Available Licence 1.0](../../LICENSE.md) replaces the
November 9 review draft. [ADR-023](../adr/ADR-023-first-party-rights-and-draft-artwork.md)
records the owner's selection: free self-builds and free unbranded community
copies, with the official name and monetization reserved. The individual author
owns the first-party rights; Univera LLC publishes the official app. Third-party
licences, artwork permissions and Store customer terms remain separate.
