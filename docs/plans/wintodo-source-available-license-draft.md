## Why this draft is source-available

The commercial restriction is deliberate. OSI's open-source definition requires
free redistribution and permits business use; this restricted licence must be
described as **source-available**, with exact self-build/redistribution rights
stated, rather than advertised as an OSI open-source licence.

Reference: [OSI Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd). GPLv3 and
MIT were considered in [the signing/licensing research](deployment-signing-licensing-recommendation.md)
and were not selected because they permit other parties' commercial copies.
