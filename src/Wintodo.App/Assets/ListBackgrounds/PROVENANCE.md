with verified **CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)** photographs from
Wikimedia Commons (see [ADR-027](../../../../docs/adr/ADR-027-mpl-2-0-open-source-license.md)).

downscaled from a native high-resolution original (≥2745 px wide, zero upscaling)
and written onto a fresh canvas with all EXIF/IPTC/XMP/comment metadata stripped
(JFIF `0xFFE0` header only).
